Worth knowing

  • Parabens: Among the most studied preservatives. Some types are banned in the UK and EU and others are permitted within limits.
  • Formaldehyde-releasing preservatives: Permitted within set limits; must carry a warning above a threshold.
  • Fragrance (parfum): If you have eczema or sensitive skin, fragrance-free is the safer choice.
