Cocoa butter for skin

What cocoa butter does for skin, what the trials say about stretch marks and scars, how it compares with shea butter, and who should keep it off their face.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Cocoa butter is a good, cheap sealant for dry body skin. That part of its reputation is earned.
  • It does not prevent stretch marks. Randomised trials found no benefit over a plain cream.
  • It can clog pores. Keep it for the body, not an acne-prone face.

Mixed evidence

What it is

Cocoa butter is the fat pressed from cocoa beans. It is solid at room temperature and melts at skin temperature, which is why it spreads so well. It is mostly saturated fats, with a little vitamin E. It softens skin and slows water loss. It does not add water, so it works best over damp skin or in a lotion that also contains glycerin.

Strong evidence

Stretch marks, scars and dark marks

Cocoa butter has been the standard advice for stretch marks for generations. When it was tested properly, including in a trial in Jamaica, women using cocoa butter developed stretch marks as often as women using a placebo cream. There is also no good evidence that it fades scars or dark marks. It makes skin feel better, which is worth something. It does not change it.

Limited evidence

Cocoa butter or shea butter

  • Cocoa butter is harder, richer, and smells of chocolate unless deodorised.
  • Shea butter is softer, less likely to clog pores, and has some evidence for calming eczema.
  • Both seal. Neither hydrates on its own.
  • For the face or spot-prone skin, shea is the safer of the two.

Mixed evidence

Cocoa butter lotions

A cocoa butter lotion or cream is water, glycerin, cocoa butter and usually petrolatum or mineral oil, often with fragrance. That mix of humectant plus sealant is why these products work well on ashy skin, more than the cocoa butter itself. Fragrance-free versions suit sensitive skin better.

When to see a doctor

A rash where you applied it, or spots that appeared after using it on your face, chest or back.

Common questions

Is cocoa butter good for your skin?

Yes, as a moisturiser for dry body skin. It seals in water and softens.

Does cocoa butter remove stretch marks?

No. Randomised trials found it no better than a placebo cream.
