Shingles on Black and brown skin

Shingles on dark skin may look purple, brown or grey, not red. The early signs, why speed matters, the vaccine, and how to limit scars and dark marks.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Pain, burning or tingling on one side of the body comes first. The rash follows days later.
  • On dark skin the patch may be purple, dark brown or grey with clear blisters, not bright red.
  • Antiviral tablets work best started within three days of the rash.

Strong evidence

What it is

Shingles is the chickenpox virus waking up in one nerve, usually decades after childhood chickenpox. It causes a band or patch of blisters on one side of the body or face, following that nerve.

Limited evidence

Spotting it on darker skin

  • A strip of skin on one side that hurts, burns or is very sensitive before anything shows.
  • Then raised patches that look darker than your skin, purple or grey.
  • Then clusters of small fluid-filled blisters, which are easy to see on any skin tone.
  • It does not cross the middle of the body.

Strong evidence

What to do

  • Speak to a pharmacist, GP or 111 the same day. In England pharmacists can assess and treat shingles in adults.
  • Start antivirals as early as possible.
  • Keep the rash clean, dry and loosely covered. Do not pick the scabs.
  • Stay away from pregnant women who have not had chickenpox, newborn babies and people with weak immunity until the blisters have crusted.

Limited evidence

Marks and scars

Shingles commonly leaves dark or light patches in darker skin, and in people prone to keloids the healed area can form raised scars. Early treatment, not picking, and sunscreen once healed all reduce that. Pain that lasts after the rash is also more likely if treatment is late.

Strong evidence

The vaccine

The NHS offers a shingles vaccine to older adults and to people with a severely weakened immune system. Eligibility by age has been changing in stages, so check the NHS page or ask your surgery.

When to see a doctor

Shingles on the face, nose or near the eye is urgent the same day: it can damage sight. Also urgent: a weak immune system, pregnancy, or a rash spreading beyond one band.

Common questions

What does shingles look like on black skin?

A band of purple, brown or grey raised patches with clusters of small blisters, on one side of the body.

Does shingles leave dark marks?

Often, in darker skin. They fade over months. Early antivirals and not picking help.
