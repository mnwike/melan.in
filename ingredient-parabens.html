Ingredients / Preservative

Parabens

Stops microbes growing in the product.

General information from cosmetic science. Not yet clinically reviewed.

Well established

What the science says

Among the most studied preservatives. Some types are banned in the UK and EU and others are permitted within limits.

Worth knowing

See the science note above.

Cancer evidence

No study has shown that parabens in cosmetics cause breast cancer. They have weak oestrogen-like activity in the laboratory, and a few longer-chain parabens are restricted or banned in the UK and EU as a precaution.

Other known downsides

Rarely cause allergy. Among the least allergenic preservatives.
