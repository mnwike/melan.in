Research / Dry skin and the skin barrier

Racial/ethnic variations in skin barrier: implications for skin care recommendations in skin of color

Andrew Alexis, and colleagues · Journal of Drugs in Dermatology, 2021

A plain-language summary by melan.in. Not written or endorsed by the authors.

What they did

An expert panel reviewed studies comparing the skin barrier, including ceramide levels and water loss, across racial and ethnic groups.

What they found

  • Some studies report lower ceramide levels and more water loss in Black skin.
  • The studies disagree with each other and many have methodological flaws.
  • Better-designed research is needed before firm conclusions are drawn.

Why it matters

Claims that Black skin is naturally drier are everywhere in marketing. This review shows the evidence is weaker than the confidence with which it is repeated.

Limits

A review with expert opinion. It cannot settle the question, only show how unsettled it is.
