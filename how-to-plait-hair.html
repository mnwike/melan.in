How to plait hair: a beginner's guide

Learn to plait hair step by step: the three-strand plait, then cornrows and two-strand twists, with tips for curly and afro hair and how to avoid tension.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Every braid style is built from one movement: cross an outside strand over the middle, then the other side.
  • Work on damp, detangled, conditioned hair. Dry coiled hair breaks when you force it.
  • Firm at the root is fine. Tight is not: if it hurts, start again looser.

Mixed evidence

Before you start

  • Wash or dampen the hair and work conditioner or a leave-in through it.
  • Detangle in sections from the ends up, with fingers then a wide-tooth comb.
  • Have clips, a rat-tail comb for parting, and a spray bottle of water to hand.

Limited evidence

The three-strand plait

  • Take a section and split it into three equal strands.
  • Cross the right strand over the middle. It is now the middle.
  • Cross the left strand over the new middle.
  • Keep alternating to the ends. Curly and coily ends often hold on their own; twirl them round a finger with a little cream.

Limited evidence

Moving on to cornrows

A cornrow is the same plait done close to the scalp. Part a narrow row. Start the plait at the hairline, and each time you cross a strand, pick up a little hair from the row beneath it. Keep your hands close to the head and the tension even. Cornrows take practice: expect the first few to be uneven.

Limited evidence

Two-strand twists

The easiest style to learn. Split a section in two and wrap one strand over the other again and again to the ends, turning each strand in the same direction as you go so the twist holds.

Strong evidence

Protecting the hairline

Dermatologists who reviewed hairstyles and hair loss advise applying braids, cornrows and twists with as little traction as possible. Leave the fine hairs at the edges out, or plait them more loosely than the rest.

When to see a doctor

Bumps, soreness or thinning at the hairline after braiding.

Common questions

Is it better to plait hair wet or dry?

Damp, with conditioner in. Coiled hair is easier to handle and breaks less.

How do I stop plaits coming undone?

Twirl the ends around a finger with a little cream or gel. Avoid tight elastic bands on the ends.
