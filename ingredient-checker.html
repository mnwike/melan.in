Ingredient checker

Paste the ingredient list from a product label. Nothing you type leaves your device.

What this checks

It looks for ingredients that UK Trading Standards say should not be in cosmetics sold over the counter, and for ingredients with evidence for dryness and dark marks. A clean result does not make a product safe: illegal creams often leave ingredients off the label. No ingredient list at all is a warning sign.

Sources: Chartered Trading Standards Institute · Davis and Callender · Our guide to skin bleaching creams
