Dr Andrew F. Alexis

Dermatologist · Weill Cornell Medicine, New York, USA

This profile points to published work. Dr Andrew F. Alexis is not affiliated with melan.in and has not reviewed this page.

Known for

Directed one of the first Skin of Color Centers for more than 15 years. He has published widely on which conditions most affect Black and brown patients, on dark marks, and on how the skin barrier is studied across populations.
