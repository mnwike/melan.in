Research / Hair shape

Shape variability and classification of human hair: a worldwide approach

Geneviève Loussouarn, Roland de la Mettrie, Didier Saint-Léger, and colleagues · Human Biology, 2007

A plain-language summary by melan.in. Not written or endorsed by the authors.

What they did

The team measured curve diameter, curl index, waves and twists in hair from 2,449 people in 22 countries, then let statistics group the results.

What they found

  • Human hair falls into eight main curl groups.
  • The groups were defined by measurement alone, without reference to ethnicity.
  • Every large population contains more than one group.

Why it matters

It gives a scientific alternative to the salon chart, and shows that curl is a spectrum shared across humanity.

Limits

It describes shape only. It does not cover porosity, thickness or how to care for each type.
