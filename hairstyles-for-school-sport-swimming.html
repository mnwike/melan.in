Hairstyles for school, sport, swimming and sleep

Which styles suit which setting for curly and afro hair: school, PE and sport, swimming, sleep, cold weather and holidays in the sun.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Pick the style for the week ahead: what will the hair go through?
  • Low-tension, low-maintenance styles win almost everywhere.
  • Water, chlorine, friction and sun are the four things to plan around.

Limited evidence

School

  • Cornrows, loose box braids, twists, or puffs and bunches held with soft bands.
  • Styles that last one to two weeks save time on school mornings.
  • If a school rule singles out braids, locs or afros, you can challenge it. Equality guidance in Britain says uniform policies should not discriminate against hairstyles linked to race.

Limited evidence

PE and sport

  • Braids or twists tied back in a low bunch. Avoid a high, tight ponytail on top of braids.
  • Wash sweat off the scalp at the next wash day; do not just add product over it.
  • For contact sports, skip beads and hard clips.

Mixed evidence

Swimming

  • Wet the hair with tap water and add conditioner before getting in: hair that is already full takes up less pool water.
  • Braids or twists under a swim cap. Large caps are made for braids, locs and afros.
  • Rinse straight after, then shampoo and condition the same day. Chlorine and salt left in dry the hair.

Limited evidence

Sleep

  • A satin or silk bonnet or pillowcase to cut friction.
  • Loose plaits or twists so the hair is not crushed and matted by morning.

Mixed evidence

Cold weather and sun

  • Winter: line wool hats with satin, and moisturise more often.
  • Strong sun: cover the hair, and protect any exposed, parted scalp.

When to see a doctor

A sore or flaky scalp under a long-term style, or thinning edges.

Common questions

Can you swim with braids?

Yes. Wet and condition them first, wear a cap if you can, and rinse and wash the same day.

What is the best hairstyle for school?

A loose, low-maintenance style that lasts a week or two, such as cornrows or twists.
