Dark knees, elbows and inner thighs

Why knees, elbows, knuckles and inner thighs are darker on Black and brown skin, which lotions even them out safely, and which creams to avoid.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Darker knees, elbows and knuckles are normal in brown and black skin. Thicker skin holds more pigment.
  • Friction and dryness make it more obvious. Softening and moisturising help most.
  • Lightening creams aimed at these areas are where illegal steroids and hydroquinone turn up.

Mixed evidence

Why these areas are darker

Skin over joints is thicker and folds constantly. Thicker skin carries more pigment, and rubbing, kneeling and leaning thicken it further. Dry, rough skin on top scatters light and looks ashy and darker at once. Inner thighs darken from skin rubbing on skin, and after shaving or ingrown hairs.

Mixed evidence

What helps

  • Daily moisturiser with urea or lactic acid, to soften the thick layer.
  • Seal with a butter or petroleum jelly at night.
  • Reduce friction: stop scrubbing, avoid leaning on elbows, wear longer shorts or anti-chafing balm for thighs.
  • Sunscreen on knees and elbows in summer.
  • Expect weeks to months. The aim is softer and more even, not a different colour.

Strong evidence

What to avoid

  • Hard scrubbing, pumice and lemon juice. Irritation makes darker skin darker.
  • 'Knuckle', 'toning' or 'triple action' creams from informal sellers. Many contain strong steroids or hydroquinone and leave stretch marks, thin skin or blue-black patches.
  • Bleach or peroxide mixtures.

Strong evidence

When it means something else

Dark, velvety, thickened skin in the neck folds, armpits and inner thighs can be acanthosis nigricans, which is linked to raised insulin and is worth a blood test. Dark patches that are itchy and scaly may be eczema or a fungal rash.

When to see a doctor

Velvety dark skin on the neck, armpits or groin, sudden darkening, or any change after using a lightening cream.

Common questions

Why are my knees and elbows darker?

The skin there is thicker and rubs constantly, so it holds more pigment. It is normal in darker skin.

How do I lighten dark knees safely?

Soften and moisturise with urea or lactic acid, cut friction, and avoid lightening creams from informal sellers.
