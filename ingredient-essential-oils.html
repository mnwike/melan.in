Ingredients / Plant oil; scent

Essential oils

Adds scent and a cooling or tingling feel.

General information from cosmetic science. Not yet clinically reviewed.

Limited evidence

What the science says

Can irritate or sensitise skin, especially undiluted. Evidence for hair growth is thin: one small 2015 trial compared rosemary oil with a low-strength hair loss medicine.

Worth knowing

See the science note above.

Cancer evidence

No established cancer link at cosmetic levels.

Other known downsides

Contact allergy and irritation, especially from tea tree, citrus and ylang-ylang oils. Citrus oils can make skin react to sunlight. Lavender and tea tree oils have been reported, in small case series, alongside early breast growth in children.
