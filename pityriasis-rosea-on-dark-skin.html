Pityriasis rosea and the herald patch

Pityriasis rosea starts with one oval herald patch, then a rash on the trunk. On dark skin it is brown, grey or purple and can leave marks. What to expect.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • One oval scaly patch appears first, the 'herald patch'. A week or two later many smaller ones follow.
  • On dark skin the patches are grey, dark brown or purple, not pink, and may be bumpier.
  • It is harmless and clears by itself in about five to eight weeks, but can leave dark or light marks.

Strong evidence

How it starts

The herald patch is a single oval, two to ten centimetres long, usually on the chest, back or tummy, with a fine collar of scale just inside its edge. It is often mistaken for ringworm. Then smaller ovals appear across the trunk, lined up along the skin creases, which on the back can look like the branches of a fir tree.

Limited evidence

How it differs on darker skin

The name means 'fine pink scale', and pink is exactly what you will not see. In Black and brown skin the patches look grey, dark brown, purple or almost black. Reports also describe more small bumps, more rash in the armpits, groin, face and neck, and more widespread rash in children of African ancestry. When it settles, darker or lighter patches can stay for months.

Mixed evidence

What causes it

Probably a reaction to a common virus of the herpes family that most people caught in childhood. It is not thought to spread between people in ordinary contact. It mostly affects people aged 10 to 35 and rarely comes back.

Mixed evidence

What helps

  • Moisturiser and lukewarm showers.
  • An antihistamine at night if the itch stops you sleeping.
  • A mild steroid cream from a pharmacist or GP for itchy areas.
  • Sunscreen on exposed patches to limit dark marks.

Strong evidence

Why it is still worth being seen

Several conditions copy it, including ringworm, a drug reaction, guttate psoriasis and secondary syphilis. A rash on the palms and soles, or one that has not faded by three months, needs a proper check and sometimes a blood test.

When to see a doctor

Pregnancy (tell your midwife or GP promptly), a rash on palms, soles or in the mouth, feeling unwell, or a rash lasting more than three months.

Common questions

What does a herald patch look like on black skin?

A single oval patch, brown, grey or purple, with fine scale just inside the edge.

Does pityriasis rosea leave marks on dark skin?

It can leave darker or lighter patches for some months. They fade.
