How to grow hair faster

Hair grows about a centimetre a month and little changes that. What limits growth, what protects length, and the truth about oils, biotin, trims and massage.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Hair grows roughly 1 to 1.5 cm a month. No product makes a healthy follicle grow faster.
  • What you can change is how much length you keep, and whether anything is slowing growth.
  • Deficiency, illness, tight styles and damage are the things worth fixing.

Strong evidence

What sets the rate

Growth rate and the length of the growing phase are set by genes, age and hormones. That is why some people can grow hair to the waist and others reach a natural limit at the shoulders.

Mixed evidence

Myths

  • Trimming does not speed growth. It removes split ends so they do not travel up the strand.
  • Biotin helps only people who lack it, and it distorts thyroid and heart blood tests.
  • Oils, rice water and onion juice do not speed growth in healthy scalps.
  • Shaving does not make hair grow back thicker.
  • Scalp massage: one small study suggested thicker hairs over months. Harmless to try.
  • Inversion and 'growth in a week' methods do nothing.

Mixed evidence

What does help

  • Enough protein and iron. Ask for a ferritin test if your periods are heavy or you eat little meat.
  • Treating dandruff and scalp inflammation.
  • Gentle handling: less heat, loose styles, satin at night, detangling wet with conditioner.
  • Minoxidil, if the real problem is pattern thinning.
  • Patience. Measure every three months, not every week.

When to see a doctor

Hair that has stopped growing in one area, patches, heavy shedding, or a sore or scaly scalp.

Common questions

How can I make my hair grow faster?

You cannot change the rate much. Protect the length you grow and fix deficiencies or scalp problems.
