Ingredients / Conditioning agent

Hydrolysed proteins and amino acids

Small protein fragments that cling to damaged areas of the hair.

General information from cosmetic science. Not yet clinically reviewed.

Some evidence

What the science says

Temporarily strengthen and smooth damaged hair. Some people find heavy use leaves hair stiff.

Cancer evidence

No cancer link.

Other known downsides

Too much protein can leave hair stiff and brittle.
