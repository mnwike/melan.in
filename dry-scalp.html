Dry scalp or dandruff?

Dry scalp and dandruff look alike but need opposite treatment. How to tell them apart on afro and curly hair, and what to do about each.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Dry scalp gives small white flakes and a tight feeling. It improves with moisture.
  • Dandruff gives larger, yellowish or greasy flakes and itch. It is driven by yeast and gets worse with heavy oil.
  • Washing more often, not less, is usually the answer to dandruff.

Mixed evidence

Telling them apart

  • Dry scalp: fine, dry, white flakes. Skin elsewhere is often dry too. Worse in winter and after harsh shampoo.
  • Dandruff or seborrhoeic dermatitis: bigger flakes that cling to the scalp, itch, sometimes at the hairline, eyebrows and behind the ears. On darker skin the patches may look lighter or darker, not red.
  • Product build-up: flakes that roll into grey balls when rubbed.
  • Psoriasis: thick, well-defined, silvery plaques that may extend past the hairline.

Limited evidence

If it is dry scalp

  • Use a gentle, sulphate-free shampoo and lukewarm water.
  • A light oil or scalp serum after washing, on damp skin.
  • A humidifier in heated rooms can help in winter.
  • Avoid scratching with nails or combs.

Strong evidence

If it is dandruff

  • An anti-dandruff shampoo with ketoconazole, selenium sulphide, zinc pyrithione or piroctone olamine.
  • Massage into the scalp, leave five minutes, rinse. Use twice a week for a month, then once a week or fortnight.
  • Follow with conditioner on the lengths, because these shampoos dry afro hair.
  • Wash at least weekly. Long gaps let yeast and oil build up.
  • Go easy on scalp oils and grease. The yeast feeds on many of them.

Mixed evidence

With braids, locs and wigs

Dilute shampoo in a nozzle bottle, apply along the partings, massage with fingertips and rinse well. Dry the scalp fully afterwards.

When to see a doctor

Flaking with hair loss, thick plaques, weeping or crusting, a flaky bald patch in a child, or no improvement after a month of medicated shampoo.

Common questions

Is my flaky scalp dry or dandruff?

Small dry white flakes suggest dry scalp. Larger, greasy, itchy flakes suggest dandruff.

Does oil help dandruff?

Usually not. Many oils feed the yeast that drives dandruff.
