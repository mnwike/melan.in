Guides, grounded in the research

Every guide gives the short answer, what the science says and how sure it is, what helps, when to see a doctor, and the papers behind it.

Strong evidence: large studies or official reviews · Mixed: small or conflicting studies · Limited: expert opinion, little data

Skin

Razor bumps and ingrown hairsRazor bumps happen when a cut hair curls back into the skin. Tightly curled hair makes this far more likely.Dark marks after spots, cuts and rashesDark marks are the skin's pigment response to inflammation. They are more common and last longer on darker skin.MelasmaMelasma is a long-term pigment condition that comes and goes. There is no permanent cure yet.Dry and ashy skinAshiness is dry, flaking surface skin. It shows more on darker tones because the flakes look grey against the skin.Eczema on darker skinOn darker skin, eczema usually looks dark brown, purple or grey, not red.Sun, sunscreen and skin cancerMelanin gives real protection, so skin cancer is less common in darker skin.Dark underarms and a dark neckDark, thickened, velvety skin in the folds is called acanthosis nigricans. It is not dirt and scrubbing will not remove it.Keloids and raised scarsA keloid is a scar that keeps growing past the edges of the wound.The essentials: a simple kit for skin and hairGood skin and hair come from a few things done steadily, not from many products.Bed bug and insect bites on dark skinOn darker skin, bites often look brown or purple, not red, and can be hard to see.Laser hair removal on dark skinIt can be safe on dark skin, with the right machine and an experienced operator.Acne on Black and brown skinIn darker skin the dark mark left behind often outlasts and outweighs the spot.Retinol and retinoids for dark skinRetinoids are among the best-evidenced treatments for dark marks and acne in skin of colour.Small dark bumps on the face (DPN)Small, smooth, dark bumps on the cheeks and around the eyes are usually DPN.Ringworm on Black and brown skinRingworm is a fungal infection. There is no worm.A dark line on a nailBrown lines running the length of the nail are common in darker skin and usually harmless.White and light patches on dark skinMost light patches are not vitiligo. They are pigment temporarily turned down after a rash, dry skin or a yeast.Pityriasis rosea and the herald patchOne oval scaly patch appears first, the 'herald patch'. The rash follows days later.Hives on Black and brown skinHives are raised, itchy weals. On dark skin they are often skin-coloured, so feel for them.Contact dermatitis on Black and brown skinA rash where something touched the skin. On dark skin it is darker, purple or grey, dry and itchy.Stretch marks on Black and brown skinNew stretch marks on dark skin are dark brown, purple or reddish-brown. Old ones turn paler than your skin.Moles, freckles and skin tags on dark skinBlack people can have freckles. They are inherited and harmless.Psoriasis on Black and brown skinOn dark skin psoriasis patches are purple, dark brown or grey, with thick silvery-grey scale.Rosacea on Black and brown skinRosacea does occur in Black and brown skin. Only a test can.Bruises and cellulitis on Black and brown skinOn dark skin a bruise looks dark purple, brown or black from the start. Bold lines and strong contrast age best.AlbinismAlbinism is inherited. A child is born with it when both parents carry the gene, usually without knowing.Body odourSweat itself barely smells. Odour comes from skin bacteria breaking it down.Acne scars and marks on dark skinFlat dark spots after acne are marks, not scars. They fade, slowly, without procedures.Dark lipsNaturally brown, purple-brown or two-toned lips are normal in darker skin.Brown spots on the skinMost brown spots on brown and black skin are marks left by spots, bites, scratches or rashes.Pigmentation on the faceThree causes account for most facial pigmentation in brown skin: melasma, marks after spots or rashes, and sun.Pimples: how to get rid of themA pimple is a blocked, inflamed pore. It needs two to three months of steady treatment, not an overnight fix.Fungal skin infections on dark skinFungal infections thrive in warmth and sweat and are common in humid climates.Keratosis pilaris on dark skinKeratosis pilaris is harmless, inherited and very common. It is not acne and not an infection.Dark inner thighsDarker inner thighs are very common in brown and black skin, and friction is the usual cause.Oily skin and large pores on darker skinPores do not open and close. Their visible size depends on oil, age and genetics.Dark skin in a cold climateWhat changes for melanin-rich skin and hair far from the equator.

Sun

World UV mapInteractive: how strong the sun is where you live, month by month.How the sun affects darker skinMelanin is a built-in filter. Deeply pigmented skin has a natural protection estimated at about SPF 13, against about SPF 3 for pale skin.Sunscreen for dark skin: how to chooseChoose broad spectrum, SPF 30 or more, in a formula you will actually wear.How the sun affects hairSunlight damages hair. UVB breaks down protein and UVA and visible light bleach the pigment.Sunburn on Black and brown skinYes, dark skin can sunburn. It takes longer and shows differently.Sunscreen for oily brown and black skinOily skin still needs sunscreen, and darker skin benefits because sun deepens dark marks.

Hair and scalp

It depends on the state of the cuticle.Do silk bonnets work?A bonnet reduces rubbing between hair and pillow and stops cotton drawing moisture out.Protective hairstyles: which ones protectA style protects only if it reduces handling without adding tension.Hair breakage: causes and fixesBreakage is hair snapping along its length. Hair loss is hair leaving the follicle. They need different answers.Hot combs, silk presses and heatHeat straightens by breaking the hair's temporary bonds. Water puts them back, which is why a press reverts in rain.Relaxers explained: lye, no-lye and texturisersAll relaxers straighten by permanently breaking bonds inside the hair. That weakens it.Going grey, and what hair dye doesGrey hair starts later, on average, in people of African descent: around the mid-forties.How to grow 4c hairYour hair is growing. If it stays the same length, it is breaking at the ends as fast as it grows.How often to wash afro and 4c hairEvery one to two weeks suits most afro-textured hair.Low porosity hairLow porosity hair takes a long time to get wet and a long time to dry.High porosity hairHigh porosity hair soaks up water quickly and dries quickly.How to detangle and moisturise 4c hairNever detangle 4c hair dry. Water and conditioner give the slip that stops it snapping.Premature grey hairGreying is mostly genetic. When it starts runs in families.Dandruff treatmentDandruff is not dryness. It is a reaction to a yeast that feeds on scalp oil.Minoxidil for hair loss in afro-textured hairMinoxidil has good evidence for pattern hair loss in men and women.Shrinkage in afro and curly hairShrinkage is normal. Coily hair can look a quarter of its real length.Going natural: transitioning and the big chopRelaxed hair cannot be turned back. It has to grow out and be cut off.Dry scalp or dandruff?Dry scalp gives small white flakes and a tight feeling. It improves with moisture.Deep conditioning and protein treatmentsConditioners coat and smooth the hair. They do not repair it permanently.

Men

Children's hair

A parent's guide to curly and coily hairCurly and coily hair is drier and more fragile than straight hair. Moisture and gentle handling solve most problems.When your child's hair is different from yoursYou do not need to have this hair to care for it well. You need a routine and a willingness to ask.Braids, extensions and tension in childrenChildren can get traction alopecia, the same hair loss from pulling that affects adults.Scalp ringworm in childrenScalp ringworm is a fungal infection, not a worm and not a sign of poor hygiene.Can Black people get head lice?Yes. Head lice are less common in afro-textured hair, not impossible.Eczema in Black and brown babiesOn dark skin eczema is brown, purple or grey, often with tiny bumps like goosebumps. Red is hard to see.Measles and chickenpox on Black and brown skinOn dark skin a measles rash may be barely visible. Feel for rough bumps and watch for the other symptoms.

Products and safety

Relaxers and your healthTwo large US studies link frequent, long-term relaxer use with a higher risk of uterine cancer.Vitamin D for people with dark skinMelanin filters the sunlight skin uses to make vitamin D.Shea butter for skin: what it can and cannot doShea butter is an emollient: it softens skin and slows water loss. That part is well accepted.Cancer and hair and skin products: the evidenceThe strongest signal is for frequent, long-term use of chemical hair straighteners and womb cancer.Vitamins for hair and skin: what worksSupplements fix deficiencies. They do little for people who are not deficient.Hydroquinone creamIn the UK hydroquinone is a prescription-only medicine at any strength. It is banned from shop cosmetics.Chemical peels on Black and brown skinSuperficial peels are generally safe on darker skin in experienced hands.

The big conversations

Skin bleaching: what is in the creamsMany skin-lightening creams sold informally in the UK contain ingredients that are illegal in cosmetics.ColourismColourism is the favouring of lighter skin over darker skin, often within the same ethnic group.Can Black people have red, blonde or straight hair?Yes to all three. Natural red, blonde and loosely curled or straight hair occur in Black people.Why is afro hair curly?Hair curls because the follicle it grows from is curved. Tightly coiled hair comes from a strongly curved follicle.Can you increase melanin?No food, vitamin or pill has been shown to increase melanin in healthy skin or hair.Colours and make-up for brown and black skinUndertone matters more than depth. Brown and black skin can be warm, cool, neutral or olive.What is melanin?Melanin is a pigment made by cells called melanocytes. Everyone has it.

Skin and hair compared

How skin differs between populationsEveryone has about the same number of pigment cells. The difference is how much melanin they make and how it is packaged.How hair differs between populationsThe chemistry of hair is the same in everyone. The shape is what differs.African and European skin and hair comparedThe two differ most in pigment and in hair shape. Almost everything else follows from those two facts.African, East Asian and South Asian skin and hair comparedAll three share a tendency to dark marks after inflammation. They differ a great deal in hair.Fitzpatrick skin types I to VIThe scale has six types, from I (always burns) to VI (never burns, deeply pigmented).

It is not dirt and it is not a skin condition.Winter lotion for Black and brown skinWinter air holds little water and heating holds less. Skin loses moisture faster than in summer.Summer lotion with SPF for Black and brown skinYes, darker skin benefits from sun protection, mostly to prevent dark marks and uneven tone.Cocoa butter for skinCocoa butter is a good, cheap sealant for dry body skin. It is cheap and sold in pharmacies.Glycerin for skinGlycerin pulls water into the outer layer of skin and holds it there. Hair oils and remedies

None.Rosemary oil for hairRosemary oil has more evidence than most hair oils, which is still only one small trial.Castor oil and Jamaican black castor oilThere is no clinical evidence that castor oil, black or clear, makes hair grow.Rice water for hairNo clinical trial has tested rice water for hair growth.Chebe powderChebe does not make hair grow. It is used to stop hair breaking, so length is kept.Black seed oil for hair and skinA few small studies suggest black seed oil may help some scalp and skin conditions. None is strong.Coconut oil for hair and skinCoconut oil is one of the best-studied hair oils. It cannot lighten hair or dye it black by itself.How to grow hair fasterHair grows roughly 1 to 1.5 cm a month. No product makes a healthy follicle grow faster.Amla and bhringraj oil for hairEvidence for both comes from laboratory and animal studies.
