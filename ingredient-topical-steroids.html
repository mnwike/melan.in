Corticosteroids

Reduces inflammation; misused to lighten skin.

General information from cosmetic science. Not yet clinically reviewed.

Well established

What the science says

Long use thins the skin and causes stretch marks and acne.

Not permitted in UK cosmetics

Not permitted in cosmetics. Prescription or pharmacy only.

Cancer evidence

No established cancer link.

Other known downsides

Skin thinning, stretch marks, acne, visible veins and, with heavy use over large areas, hormone problems. Stopping suddenly can cause a severe flare.
