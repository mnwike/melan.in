Ingredients / Scent

Fragrance (parfum)

Makes the product smell pleasant. A single word can cover dozens of scent chemicals.

General information from cosmetic science. Not yet clinically reviewed.

Well established

What the science says

Fragrance is one of the most common causes of cosmetic contact allergy. Most people tolerate it.

Worth knowing

If you have eczema or sensitive skin, fragrance-free is the safer choice.

Cancer evidence

No established cancer link for fragrance as such. 'Parfum' can hide dozens of chemicals. Some once-common fragrance chemicals, such as lilial, have been banned for harming fertility, and some fragrances contain phthalates, which are being studied for effects on hormones.

Other known downsides

The commonest cause of cosmetic contact allergy. Can worsen eczema and leave dark marks after a reaction.
