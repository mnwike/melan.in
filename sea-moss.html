Sea moss for skin and hair

Sea moss gel is sold for glowing skin, hair growth and '92 minerals'. What it is, what the evidence supports, and the real iodine and heavy metal risks.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • No human trial shows that sea moss improves skin or grows hair.
  • It is a seaweed rich in iodine. Too much iodine can upset the thyroid.
  • As a gel on skin it is a mild moisturiser, nothing more.

Strong evidence

What it is

Sea moss is a name used for several red seaweeds: Chondrus crispus from the cold Atlantic, known as Irish moss, and Gracilaria and Eucheuma from warm waters, which is what most Caribbean sea moss is. In Jamaica, Trinidad and St Lucia it has long been boiled into a thick drink with milk and spices. It is the source of carrageenan, a thickener used in food.

Limited evidence

The claims and the evidence

  • '92 of the 102 minerals the body needs': there is no such list of 102, and no analysis supports the figure.
  • Skin: no trials. Carrageenan gel holds water, so it can soften skin for a while, like aloe.
  • Hair growth: no trials.
  • Gut health and immunity: laboratory and animal work only.
  • It does contain iodine, some iron, magnesium and fibre. Amounts vary widely between batches.

Mixed evidence

The risks

  • Iodine: a few spoonfuls of gel can exceed the daily need. Too much can cause an overactive or underactive thyroid, especially in people with thyroid disease.
  • Seaweed takes up arsenic, lead, cadmium and mercury from the water it grows in. Home-made and unbranded gels are not tested.
  • Gel is a moist food with no preservative. Keep it in the fridge and discard it after two to three weeks, or sooner if it smells.
  • Avoid in pregnancy unless your midwife or doctor agrees, because of the iodine.

Limited evidence

If you want to use it

  • Treat it as a food you enjoy, not a medicine. One to two tablespoons of gel a day at most.
  • Buy from a seller who publishes heavy metal test results.
  • On skin, patch test first and rinse off after ten to fifteen minutes.

When to see a doctor

A racing heart, weight change, a swollen neck, or tiredness after starting sea moss: stop and ask for a thyroid blood test.

Common questions

Does sea moss help skin?

There are no human trials. The gel is a mild moisturiser on the surface of the skin.

Can you take sea moss every day?

A small amount is likely fine for most adults, but the iodine can upset the thyroid, so avoid large or daily heavy use.
