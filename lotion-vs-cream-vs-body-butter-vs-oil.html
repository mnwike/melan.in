Lotion, cream, body butter or body oil?

The difference between body lotion, cream, body butter and body oil, which suits dry and ashy skin, and how to layer them through a British year.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • They differ in how much water and how much oil they contain. That is all.
  • Lotion is mostly water. Cream is half and half. Butter and oil contain little or no water.
  • Oil and butter seal. They do not hydrate. Put them over damp skin or over a lotion.

Strong evidence

The four types

  • Lotion: mostly water, light, sinks in fast. For summer, humid weather and oily or normal skin.
  • Cream: more oil, comes in a tub or tube. For dry skin and for winter.
  • Body butter: mostly butters and oils, thick. For very dry skin, knees, elbows and feet.
  • Body oil: no water. For sealing and shine.

Mixed evidence

Why oil alone leaves you ashy

Oil gives instant shine, so it looks like it has fixed dryness. But the dryness is a lack of water in the outer layer, and oil contains none. Once it rubs off, the ash is back. Oil on damp skin straight from the shower, or over a lotion, works far better than oil on dry skin.

Limited evidence

Layering

  • Thinnest first: lotion or cream on damp skin.
  • Then oil or butter on top, where you need it.
  • In summer the first layer is usually enough.
  • In winter most darker skin does better with both.

Limited evidence

Shimmer and glow oils

Shimmer oils and glow lotions add fine mineral particles that reflect light. On deep skin tones, gold and bronze tones flatter where silver and pearl can look grey. They are make-up for the body, not skincare, and come off on clothes.

When to see a doctor

Spots on the chest or back after switching to a heavy butter or oil, or dryness that no product improves.

Common questions

Is body butter better than lotion?

For very dry skin and cold weather, yes. For summer or oily skin a lotion is enough.

Should I use body oil or lotion?

Lotion adds water. Oil seals it in. For dry skin use lotion first, then oil.
