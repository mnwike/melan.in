Why is afro hair curly?

Afro hair coils because of the shape of the follicle. The genes involved, and the evidence that tightly curled hair evolved to keep the brain cool in the sun.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Hair curls because the follicle it grows from is curved. Tightly coiled hair comes from a strongly curved follicle.
  • Many genes shape curl. No single 'afro hair gene' exists.
  • The best current evidence is that tight curls evolved to protect the head from the sun's heat.

Strong evidence

The follicle

A straight follicle produces a round, straight fibre. A curved, hook-shaped follicle produces a flattened fibre that coils as it grows, with the cells on the inside and outside of the curve built differently. The tighter the curve, the tighter the coil.

Mixed evidence

The genes

Studies in South African, Latin American and other populations have linked curl to variants in several genes that build the inner sheath of the follicle and the hair fibre, including TCHH and KRT74. Curl is inherited from many genes acting together, which is why siblings can have different textures.

Mixed evidence

Why it evolved

In 2023 researchers put wigs of different textures on a heated model of a human head under simulated sunlight. Tightly curled hair let the least solar heat reach the scalp and reduced the sweat needed to stay cool. Early humans walking upright in equatorial Africa had a large, heat-sensitive brain directly under the sun. Tight curls, standing off the scalp with air between them, look like a built-in sun shield.

Strong evidence

What it means day to day

The same shape that shields the scalp makes the fibre drier and easier to break, because scalp oil does not travel down a coil and each bend is a weak point. Afro hair care is mostly about working with that geometry.

Common questions

Why is African hair so curly?

The follicle is strongly curved, which makes the fibre coil. Tight curls appear to have evolved to keep the head cool under strong sun.
