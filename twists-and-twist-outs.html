Twists and twist-outs

Two strand twists, mini twists, passion and Senegalese twists, and twist-outs. How to install them without tension, how long to keep them, and scalp care.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Twists on your own hair are among the gentlest protective styles.
  • Twists with added hair carry the same traction risks as braids.
  • Six to eight weeks is the usual limit for twists with extensions.

Strong evidence

The types

  • Two strand twists: your own hair, two sections wound round each other.
  • Mini twists: the same, very small. Can last three to four weeks.
  • Twist-out: twists taken down after drying for a defined, stretched curl.
  • Senegalese or rope twists: smooth synthetic hair added.
  • Passion twists and spring twists: curly or springy synthetic hair, lighter and looser.
  • Marley or Havana twists: thick, textured hair for a chunkier look.

Mixed evidence

Installing them kindly

  • Start on clean, conditioned, detangled hair.
  • Twist on damp hair with a leave-in and a little cream or butter.
  • Do not pull at the roots. You should be able to move your scalp and raise your eyebrows without pain.
  • Bigger sections and lighter hair mean less weight on each follicle.
  • Leave the fine hairs at the edges out.

Mixed evidence

Looking after them

  • Wash the scalp every one to two weeks with diluted shampoo, then dry fully.
  • Mist with water or a light leave-in. Oil the scalp only if it is dry, not flaky.
  • Sleep in a satin bonnet or scarf.
  • Take them down gently with conditioner or oil, and detangle before washing, or the shed hair will mat.

Limited evidence

A good twist-out

  • Twist on damp, not soaking, hair.
  • Let it dry completely before unravelling.
  • Oil your fingertips, unravel, then separate each twist once or twice.
  • Re-twist in a few large sections at night to keep it for several days.

When to see a doctor

Pain, bumps or pus along the hairline or partings, a widening parting, or patches where hair is not growing back. Take the style out and see a GP or dermatologist.

Common questions

How long can I keep twists in?

Up to six to eight weeks with extensions. Mini twists on your own hair, about three to four weeks.

Do twists help hair grow?

They do not speed growth. They reduce handling, so less hair breaks.
