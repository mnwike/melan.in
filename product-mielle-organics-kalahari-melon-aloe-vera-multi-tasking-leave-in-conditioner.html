Worth knowing

  • Short-chain alcohols: Not the same as fatty alcohols such as cetearyl alcohol, which are conditioning.
  • Fragrance (parfum): If you have eczema or sensitive skin, fragrance-free is the safer choice.
  • Listed fragrance allergens: UK and EU rules require these to be listed by name above a set level.
  • Essential oils: Can irritate or sensitise skin, especially undiluted. Evidence for hair growth is thin: one small 2015 trial compared rosemary oil with a low-strength hair loss medicine.

A note here is not a verdict on the product. It tells you what to look up.
