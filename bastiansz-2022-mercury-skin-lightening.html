Research / Skin lightening

A systematic review of mercury exposures from skin-lightening products

Ashley Bastiansz, and colleagues · Environmental Health Perspectives, 2022

A plain-language summary by melan.in. Not written or endorsed by the authors.

What they did

A systematic review that pooled studies measuring mercury in skin-lightening products and in the bodies of people who use them, across many countries.

What they found

  • Mercury is added to some lightening products because it blocks melanin production.
  • Products exceeding the international limit of one part per million are still on sale around the world.
  • Users of these products show raised mercury levels.

Why it matters

It shows that mercury in lightening creams is a current, global problem and not a historical one.

Limits

The pooled studies used different methods and sampled products in different ways, so prevalence figures vary widely.
