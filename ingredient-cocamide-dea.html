Ingredients / Foam booster

Cocamide DEA and other diethanolamines

Thickens shampoos and makes them foam.

General information from cosmetic science. Not yet clinically reviewed.

Some evidence

What the science says

Caused cancer in animal studies when applied to skin at high doses. It can also react with other ingredients to form nitrosamines.

Restricted

Classed as possibly carcinogenic to humans. Restricted in UK and EU cosmetics. Cocamide MEA and MIPA are different chemicals and are not classed this way.

Cancer evidence

Classed as possibly carcinogenic to humans on the basis of animal studies. There is no evidence from human studies.

Other known downsides

Can irritate skin. May form nitrosamines with some other ingredients.

How it appears on a label

Look for: cocamide dea, lauramide dea, diethanolamine, cocamide diethanolamine.
