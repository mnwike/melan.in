Ringworm on Black and brown skin

Ringworm on dark skin is often brown or grey, not red, and the centre may stay dark. How to spot it, treat it from a pharmacy, and avoid the usual mistake.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Ringworm is a fungal infection. There is no worm.
  • On dark skin the ring is often brown, grey or purple, and the middle may stay dark instead of clearing.
  • Antifungal cream from a pharmacy treats most cases. Steroid cream alone makes it worse.

Limited evidence

What it looks like on darker skin

Textbooks describe a red ring with a clear centre. On Black and brown skin the edge is more often brown, grey or dark purple, slightly raised and scaly. The centre can stay darker than the skin around it, because the inflammation leaves pigment behind, so the 'ring' can look like a solid dark patch with a rough rim. Run a finger over it: the scaly, raised border is the clue.

Limited evidence

What it gets mistaken for

  • Eczema, especially round 'discoid' eczema, which is scaly all over rather than at the rim.
  • Pityriasis rosea, which starts with one oval patch and then spreads as many small ones.
  • Psoriasis, which has thicker scale.
  • A dark mark left by something else. Dark marks are flat and not scaly.

Strong evidence

Treating it

  • Use an antifungal cream such as terbinafine or clotrimazole from a pharmacy.
  • Cover the patch and a margin of normal skin around it.
  • Keep going for one to two weeks after it looks clear, or it returns.
  • Wash towels and bedding, and do not share them.
  • Check pets. A kitten or puppy with a bald patch is a common source.

Strong evidence

The mistake that makes it spread

Steroid creams calm the itch and fade the ring, so it looks better while the fungus spreads underneath. This includes the strong steroid and mixed 'triple action' creams sold informally as skin lighteners. If a patch keeps growing despite a cream, tell the pharmacist exactly what you have been using.

Mixed evidence

The mark it leaves

After the fungus has gone, a dark or sometimes light patch can remain for months. That is a mark, not active infection, and it does not need more antifungal. It fades on its own. Sunscreen helps it fade evenly.

When to see a doctor

Ringworm on the scalp or in the beard (this needs tablets, not cream), many patches, no improvement after two weeks of antifungal cream, or a weakened immune system.

Common questions

What does ringworm look like on black skin?

A round patch with a raised, scaly rim that is brown, grey or purple rather than red. The centre may stay dark.

Does ringworm leave a dark mark?

Often, yes. The mark is flat, not scaly, and fades over some months.
