Where on your head is the problem? Different conditions favour different places, which is why a doctor always asks.

Three rules that hold everywhere

  • Pain, pus, bumps or a burning scalp with hair loss means see a doctor. Those are signs of inflammation that can scar.
  • Hair loss treated early can usually be stopped. Scarred follicles do not regrow.
  • A map is not a diagnosis. It tells you what to ask about.
