Minoxidil for hair loss in afro-textured hair

Minoxidil is the only widely available treatment with good evidence for pattern hair loss. How it works, what to expect, and how to use it on afro hair.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Minoxidil has good evidence for pattern hair loss in men and women.
  • It takes four to six months to show, and the benefit is lost if you stop.
  • It does not treat scarring hair loss such as CCCA alone. That needs a dermatologist early.

Strong evidence

What it does

Minoxidil was a blood pressure tablet that turned out to grow hair. On the scalp it lengthens the growth phase of the hair cycle and thickens fine hairs. In the UK it is sold in pharmacies as a 2% or 5% liquid and a 5% foam. Dermatologists also prescribe low-dose tablets off-licence.

Strong evidence

What to expect

  • Extra shedding in the first four to six weeks is common and settles.
  • Visible change at four to six months. Full effect by about a year.
  • It works only while you use it. Hair returns to where it would have been within three to six months of stopping.
  • It does not work for everyone.

Mixed evidence

Using it with afro-textured hair

  • Foam is usually easier than liquid. The liquid contains propylene glycol, which often causes itching and flaking.
  • Part the hair and apply to the scalp, not the hair.
  • It can leave hair dry and make a silk press revert. Many people apply it at night, and moisturise lengths separately.
  • With braids, wigs or locs, use a nozzle or dropper to reach the scalp.
  • Wash hands after. It can grow fine hair on the forehead and cheeks if it runs.

Mixed evidence

Edges and traction

For thinning edges from tight styles, the first treatment is to stop the pulling. Dermatologists sometimes add minoxidil while follicles are still alive. Once the skin is smooth and shiny, the follicles have scarred and it will not help.

Strong evidence

Who should check first

  • Pregnant or breastfeeding: do not use.
  • Heart disease or low blood pressure: ask a doctor, especially about tablets.
  • Under 18: ask a doctor.
  • Keep it away from cats and dogs. It is toxic to them.

When to see a doctor

A sore, tender or burning scalp, hair loss at the crown that spreads outward, or bald patches. These can be scarring conditions that need treatment quickly. On tablets: dizziness, swollen ankles, a fast heartbeat or chest pain.

Common questions

Does minoxidil work on Black hair?

Yes. It acts on the follicle, which works the same way in every hair type. The foam is usually easier to use on afro-textured hair.

Will minoxidil regrow my edges?

Only if the follicles are still alive and the pulling has stopped. It cannot regrow hair from scarred skin.
