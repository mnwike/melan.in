Women's hair and skin after 40

What perimenopause and menopause do to hair and skin, what is different for darker skin and coily hair, and which treatments have evidence.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Falling oestrogen dries the skin, thins it and slows healing. Hair gets finer and the parting widens.
  • Darker skin wrinkles later but shows uneven tone and dryness sooner.
  • Minoxidil, retinoids, moisturiser and sunscreen have evidence. Most 'menopause' supplements do not.

Strong evidence

What changes

Menopause usually arrives between 45 and 55. Skin becomes drier and itchier, thinner and slower to heal. One estimate is that women lose about 30% of the collagen in their skin in the first five years after menopause. Up to half of women develop female pattern hair loss in their lives, and the years around menopause are when it shows most: a widening parting and thinner hair on top, with the front hairline kept.

Mixed evidence

What is different for us

Scarring hair loss at the crown, called CCCA, mostly affects women of African descent over 30, so thinning at the crown should not be put down to 'just the menopause' without a look from a doctor. Years of relaxers, heat and tight styles add up at the same age. Dark marks and melasma can deepen as skin dries and heals more slowly.

Strong evidence

What helps skin

  • A plain, rich moisturiser twice a day, and a gentle non-soap cleanser.
  • Daily tinted sunscreen for tone.
  • A retinoid at night for texture and collagen. Start low and slow to avoid irritation and marks.
  • Hormone replacement therapy increases skin thickness and collagen. That is a conversation for your GP, weighed with everything else HRT does.

Strong evidence

What helps hair

  • Minoxidil 2% or 5% on the scalp can stop loss or bring some regrowth in about six in ten women with pattern hair loss.
  • A blood test for iron, vitamin D and thyroid if shedding is sudden.
  • Looser styles, less heat, longer gaps between relaxers, or none.
  • More conditioning: grey and ageing hair is drier.

When to see a doctor

A widening bald patch at the crown, a burning or tender scalp, sudden heavy shedding, or bleeding after the menopause.

Common questions

Does menopause cause hair loss?

It often unmasks female pattern hair loss: a wider parting and thinner hair on top.

What is the best skincare for menopause?

Moisturiser, daily sunscreen and a retinoid have the best evidence.
