Professor Nina Jablonski

Anthropologist · Pennsylvania State University, USA

This profile points to published work. Professor Nina Jablonski is not affiliated with melan.in and has not reviewed this page.

Known for

With George Chaplin, used satellite measurements of ultraviolet light to explain why human skin colour varies across the planet. Her writing has done much to separate the biology of skin colour from the idea of race.
