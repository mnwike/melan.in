Common dermatologic disorders in skin of color: a comparative practice survey

Andrew Alexis, Amanda Sergay, Susan Taylor · Cutis, 2007

A plain-language summary by melan.in. Not written or endorsed by the authors.

What they did

The team reviewed the diagnoses made at visits to a New York Skin of Color Center and compared those for Black patients with those for white patients.

What they found

  • The leading diagnoses differed between the two groups.
  • For Black patients the list was headed by acne, then pigment problems (dyschromia), then eczema.
  • Pigment problems and hair loss were common in Black patients and did not reach the top ten for white patients.

Why it matters

It put numbers on something patients already knew: uneven pigment and hair loss are central concerns for Black patients, and services built around other priorities miss them.

Limits

One clinic in one city, so the mix of conditions may differ elsewhere.
