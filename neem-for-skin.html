Neem for skin and scalp

Neem soap, neem oil and neem leaf paste are used across India and West Africa for spots, rashes and dandruff. What the evidence supports and what is unsafe.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Neem has antibacterial and antifungal effects in the laboratory. Human skin trials are few.
  • Neem soap is a reasonable cleanser. Neem oil is a common cause of skin allergy.
  • Neem oil must never be swallowed, and never given to children.

Strong evidence

What it is

Neem, Azadirachta indica, is a tree native to the Indian subcontinent and now grown across West and East Africa, where it is called dogoyaro in Nigeria and mwarobaini in Swahili. Leaves, bark and seed oil are used in Ayurvedic and African traditional medicine, and the oil is a registered insecticide.

Limited evidence

The evidence

  • Laboratory: extracts slow the growth of acne bacteria, some fungi and head lice.
  • Dandruff and scalp: small studies of neem shampoos, usually mixed with other ingredients.
  • Acne, eczema, ringworm, dark marks: no good human trials.
  • Head lice: some neem-based lotions have trial support, but products vary.

Limited evidence

Using it

  • Soap or face wash: fine for oily skin. Stop if skin feels tight or dry.
  • Oil: always dilute, a few drops in a tablespoon of carrier oil. Patch test for two days.
  • Leaf paste: rinse off after ten to fifteen minutes.
  • Do not use on broken skin or on babies.

Strong evidence

Safety

  • Swallowed neem oil has caused seizures, liver damage and deaths in young children.
  • Avoid in pregnancy and when trying to conceive.
  • Contact allergy is well recognised. A rash that spreads beyond where you applied it means stop.

When to see a doctor

A ring-shaped rash, a scaly patch of hair loss in a child, or spots that scar. These need proper antifungal or acne treatment.

Common questions

Is neem good for pimples?

It kills acne bacteria in the laboratory, but there are no good human trials. Proven treatments work better.

Is neem oil safe?

On skin, diluted, for most adults. It must never be swallowed.
