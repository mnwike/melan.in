Research / Relaxers and cancer

Hair relaxer use and risk of uterine cancer in the Black Women's Health Study

Kimberly Bertrand, Julie Palmer, Lynn Rosenberg, and colleagues · Environmental Research, 2023

A plain-language summary by melan.in. Not written or endorsed by the authors.

What they did

The team followed 44,798 Black women with no history of cancer and compared uterine cancer rates by how long and how often they had used relaxers.

What they found

  • Among women past the menopause, long-term use was linked with a higher rate of uterine cancer.
  • Among women before the menopause there was no clear link.
  • Across all heavy users the increase was not statistically certain.

Why it matters

It tested the earlier finding in the group that uses relaxers most, and found the signal mainly in older women with long-term use.

Limits

Use was self-reported and products were not identified. An association, not proof of cause.
