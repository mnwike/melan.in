Hair oils and remedies: the evidence

Every culture with textured or dark hair has its own oil. Here is what each one is, where it comes from, and what the evidence does and does not show.

The evidence at a glance

What each remedy has behind it for hair growth, and what it reliably does. "None" means no human study exists, not that it has been disproved.
