People

Professor Ncoza Dlova

Dermatologist · University of KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa

This profile points to published work. Professor Ncoza Dlova is not affiliated with melan.in and has not reviewed this page.

Known for

A leading South African dermatologist who has headed dermatology at UKZN. Her doctoral research mapped skin and hair disorders in Black South Africans and analysed what is in skin-lightening products. She was part of the team that linked the PADI3 gene to scarring hair loss.
