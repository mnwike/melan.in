Research / Hair growth and density worldwide

Diversity in human hair growth, diameter, colour and shape

Genevieve Loussouarn · European Journal of Dermatology, 2016

A plain-language summary by melan.in. Not written or endorsed by the authors.

What they did

Researchers measured hair density, growth rate and the share of resting hairs on three areas of the scalp in 2,249 young adults from 24 ethnic groups on five continents.

What they found

  • Hair growth varied widely across the whole group.
  • Scalp area, sex and geographic origin all made a difference.
  • Overall, African hair showed lower density and slower growth.

Why it matters

It is the largest set of measurements of how hair actually grows in different populations, and it shows how much overlap there is.

Limits

Young adults without hair loss only. Industry-run. Group labels are broad, and averages hide wide individual ranges.
