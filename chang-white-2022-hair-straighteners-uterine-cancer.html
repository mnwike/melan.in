Research / Relaxers and cancer

Use of straighteners and other hair products and incident uterine cancer

Che-Jung Chang, Alexandra White, and colleagues · Journal of the National Cancer Institute, 2022

A plain-language summary by melan.in. Not written or endorsed by the authors.

What they did

Researchers at the US National Institutes of Health followed 33,497 women in the Sister Study for about 11 years and recorded which hair products they used.

What they found

  • 378 women developed uterine cancer during the study.
  • An estimated 1.64% of women who never used straighteners would develop uterine cancer by age 70.
  • For frequent users, more than four times a year, the estimate was 4.05%.

Why it matters

It was the first large study to link straighteners with uterine cancer. Black women use these products more, so any risk falls on them most.

Limits

An association, not proof of cause. Brands and ingredients were not recorded, and all participants had a sister with breast cancer.
