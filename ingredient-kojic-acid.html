Ingredients / Skin-care active

Kojic acid, arbutin and liquorice

Slow the enzyme that makes melanin.

General information from cosmetic science. Not yet clinically reviewed.

Some evidence

What the science says

Listed in dermatology reviews as options for dark marks, with less evidence than hydroquinone.

Cancer evidence

Not classifiable as to cancer in humans (Group 3). Limited to low concentrations in UK and EU cosmetics.

Other known downsides

Contact allergy and irritation in some people.
