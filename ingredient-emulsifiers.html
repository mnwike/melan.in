Ingredients / Emulsifier

Emulsifiers

Keeps oil and water mixed.

General information from cosmetic science. Not yet clinically reviewed.

Well established

What the science says

Needed in almost every cream. Well tolerated.

Cancer evidence

No cancer link for the ingredients themselves. Ethoxylated ones (PEGs and names ending in '-eth') can carry traces of 1,4-dioxane, a possible carcinogen, if poorly purified.

Other known downsides

Occasional irritation.

Cancer and hair and skin products: the evidence

How it appears on a label

Look for: glyceryl stearate, ceteareth, polysorbate, peg-, ppg-, steareth, laureth, oleth.
