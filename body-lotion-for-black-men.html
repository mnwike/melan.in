Body lotion for Black men

Body lotion for Black and brown men: why ash shows, what to look for, where to put it, and how it fits with shaving, the gym and a two-minute routine.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Men's skin is slightly thicker and oilier, but it dries and ashes in the same way.
  • A 'for men' label changes the scent and the price. The ingredients are the same.
  • Hands, elbows, knees, shins and a shaved head are the places that show it.

Mixed evidence

What is different about men's skin

On average men have thicker skin and make more oil on the face, chest and back. Legs, arms and hands are as dry as anyone's. Many men were never taught to moisturise, and wash with bar soap and hot water, which makes dryness worse.

Mixed evidence

What to look for

  • Glycerin or urea near the top of the list.
  • A texture you will actually use: lotion for quick absorption, cream for winter.
  • Unscented, or lightly scented, so it does not fight with aftershave.
  • A big pump bottle by the shower works better than a small tub in a drawer.

Limited evidence

A two-minute routine

  • Out of the shower, pat dry.
  • Lotion on arms, legs, hands and feet while skin is damp.
  • Extra on elbows, knees and knuckles.
  • Shaved head: a light lotion, and sunscreen in summer.
  • After the gym: shower soon, then moisturise. Sweat left on skin feeds body acne.

Mixed evidence

Shaving and lotion

Heavy butters and oils on a freshly shaved beard area or head can block follicles and worsen razor bumps. Use a light, fragrance-free moisturiser there, and keep the thick products for the body.

When to see a doctor

Bumps on the back of the scalp or beard area, dark thickened skin on the neck, or cracks on the hands or feet that will not heal.

Common questions

Do men need a different body lotion?

No. The ingredients that work are the same. Choose by texture and scent.

What is the best lotion for Black men?

A glycerin or urea lotion used daily after showering, with something thicker on elbows, knees and hands.
