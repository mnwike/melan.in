Ingredients / Skin-lightening medicine

Hydroquinone

Blocks the enzyme that makes melanin.

General information from cosmetic science. Not yet clinically reviewed.

Well established

What the science says

An effective prescription treatment for dark marks and melasma under medical supervision. Long unsupervised use can cause permanent blue-black darkening.

Not permitted in UK cosmetics

Banned from cosmetics in the UK and EU. Legal only on prescription.

How it appears on a label

Look for: hydroquinone.
