Amla and bhringraj oil for hair

Amla and bhringraj are the two best-known Ayurvedic hair oils. What each plant is, what studies exist, how to use them, and what they cannot do.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Evidence for both comes from laboratory and animal studies. Human trials are few and small.
  • As oils they reduce friction and breakage, which helps hair keep its length.
  • Neither reverses greying or regrows hair lost to scarring or pattern hair loss.

Strong evidence

What they are

Amla is the Indian gooseberry, Phyllanthus emblica, a sour fruit very high in vitamin C. Bhringraj is false daisy, Eclipta alba or Eclipta prostrata, a small herb whose name is often translated as 'king of hair'. In both cases the plant is steeped in a carrier oil, usually coconut or sesame, and often blended with brahmi, hibiscus or fenugreek. Many shop-bought 'amla oils' are mostly mineral oil with fragrance and colour.

Limited evidence

What studies show

  • Bhringraj: extracts made mouse hair enter its growth phase faster in several studies. No good human trial.
  • Amla: laboratory studies suggest an effect on an enzyme linked to pattern hair loss. Small human studies of mixed herbal products cannot separate amla from the rest.
  • Greying: no evidence that either darkens hair. Any darkening is a stain on the surface.
  • Coconut oil base: reduces protein loss from hair. This part is well supported.

Limited evidence

How to use them

  • Warm a little and massage into the scalp and lengths for a few minutes.
  • Leave for thirty minutes to overnight, then shampoo out. Tightly coiled hair usually tolerates longer than fine straight hair.
  • Once or twice a week is enough. Daily heavy oiling can worsen dandruff.
  • Read the label. Choose one where the plant and carrier oil come first, not mineral oil.
  • Patch test. Bhringraj and added fragrance can cause contact allergy.

When to see a doctor

Hair coming out in handfuls, bald patches, a sore or scaly scalp, or a rash after using an oil. No oil treats scarring hair loss, and delay costs hair.

Common questions

Does bhringraj oil regrow hair?

It has only been shown to do so in mice. There is no good human trial.

Does amla oil turn grey hair black?

No. It may stain hair slightly, but it does not restore pigment.
