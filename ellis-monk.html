Professor Ellis Monk

Sociologist · Harvard University, USA

This profile points to published work. Professor Ellis Monk is not affiliated with melan.in and has not reviewed this page.

Known for

Studies colourism and its effect on health and life chances. He created the ten-tone Monk Skin Tone scale, an open alternative to older scales, used to check that technology works across skin tones.

More on this work
