Hair breakage: causes and fixes

Why curly and afro-textured hair breaks, how to tell breakage from hair loss, and the changes that reduce it, according to dermatologists.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Breakage is hair snapping along its length. Hair loss is hair leaving the follicle. They need different answers.
  • Coiled hair is fragile at every bend and dry along its length, so rough handling shows quickly.
  • Most breakage comes from combing dry, heat, chemicals and tension.

Mixed evidence

Breakage or hair loss?

Broken hairs are short, uneven and have no bulb at the end. Shed hairs are full length with a small white bulb. Patches of bare scalp or a receding hairline point to hair loss, which needs a doctor.

Strong evidence

Why textured hair breaks

Tightly coiled hair is intrinsically more fragile because of its shape and its tendency to form knots. Dermatologists note that it can break even with ordinary styling.

Mixed evidence

What reduces it

  • Detangle wet with conditioner. Combing wet coiled hair takes less force than combing it dry.
  • Stop bleaching, and swap permanent dye for semi-permanent or temporary colour.
  • Use a heat protectant, and use heat less.
  • Loosen tight styles.
  • Trim damaged ends so splits do not travel up.
  • Sleep on satin or silk.

When to see a doctor

Breakage with a sore, scaly or itchy scalp, or hair that snaps off in distinct patches.

Common questions

How do I stop my hair breaking?

Detangle it wet with conditioner, cut down on heat, bleach and tight styles, and trim damaged ends.

Is breakage the same as hair loss?

No. Breakage is the strand snapping. Hair loss is the hair falling from the root.
