Research / Skin and hair disorders in Africa

Ethnic skin and hair disorders in KwaZulu-Natal (PhD thesis)

Ncoza Dlova · University of KwaZulu-Natal, 2014

A plain-language summary by melan.in. Not written or endorsed by the authors.

What they did

A doctoral study of the range of skin and hair disorders in Black patients in KwaZulu-Natal, together with the make-up and use of skin lighteners, traditional cosmetics and sunscreen.

What they found

  • The five most common problems were acne, eczemas, pigment disorders, infections and hair disorders, in that order.
  • Clays long used in southern Africa were examined as traditional sun protection.
  • Skin-lightening products in local use were analysed for what they contain.

Why it matters

Most dermatology data come from the United States. This is detailed evidence from an African population, gathered by an African dermatologist.

Limits

A thesis from one province. Parts of it were later published as separate journal papers.
