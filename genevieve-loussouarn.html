Hair scientist · L'Oréal Research, France

This profile points to published work. Dr Geneviève Loussouarn is not affiliated with melan.in and has not reviewed this page.

Known for

Measured how hair grows and curls in populations around the world. Her team's eight-group classification describes hair by its geometry, with no reference to ethnicity.
