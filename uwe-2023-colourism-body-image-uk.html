Research / Colourism

Perceived colourism, body image and psychological wellbeing among people of colour in the UK

Researchers at the University of the West of England · Body Image, 2023

A plain-language summary by melan.in. Not written or endorsed by the authors.

What they did

A survey of 516 Black, Asian and other minority-ethnic adults in the UK about their experience of colourism and how they feel about their bodies.

What they found

  • Colourism from within a person's own community was linked with lower satisfaction with skin shade.
  • It was also linked with more monitoring of skin shade, worse body image and more psychological distress.

Why it matters

Most colourism research comes from the United States. This is UK evidence that shade prejudice affects wellbeing here too.

Limits

A one-off survey, so it shows links and cannot show which came first.
