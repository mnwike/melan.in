How often to wash afro and 4c hair

How often to wash afro-textured and 4c hair, why weekly to fortnightly suits most people, washing in braids and locs, and what a flaky scalp is telling you.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Every one to two weeks suits most afro-textured hair.
  • Less often than that, and the scalp builds up oil, product and yeast, which causes flaking and itch.
  • Wash more often if you sweat a lot, swim, or have dandruff.

Mixed evidence

Why not daily, and why not monthly

Scalp oil does not travel down coiled hair, so the lengths are naturally dry and frequent shampooing dries them more. But the scalp is skin, and it makes as much oil as anyone's. Studies of Black women link infrequent washing with more seborrhoeic dermatitis. Weekly to fortnightly balances the two.

Limited evidence

How to wash

  • Pre-treat lengths with oil or conditioner if hair is very dry.
  • Shampoo the scalp with fingertips. Let the lather rinse through the lengths.
  • Condition generously and detangle while it is in.
  • Co-washing with conditioner alone is fine between shampoos, not instead of them.
  • Dry gently with a T-shirt or microfibre towel.

Limited evidence

In braids, locs and under wigs

Dilute shampoo in an applicator bottle, work it along the parts, rinse well, and dry completely. A scalp under a wig or weave still needs washing every one to two weeks.

When to see a doctor

Thick scale, weeping, hair loss with flaking, or itch that does not settle with an anti-dandruff shampoo.

Common questions

How often should 4c hair be washed?

Every one to two weeks for most people, more often with heavy sweating or dandruff.

Sources

  • Wong et al., Evidence-based care of textured hair in Black women: a review of 29 studies (Cutis, 2025)
