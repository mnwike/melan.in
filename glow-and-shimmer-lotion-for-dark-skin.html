Glow, shimmer and gradual tan on dark skin

Glow lotions, shimmer oils and gradual tan on Black and brown skin: which shades flatter deep skin tones, what is in them, and how to get glow without glitter.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Healthy glow on dark skin is mostly hydration. Moisturised skin reflects light evenly.
  • For shimmer, gold, bronze and copper tones suit deep skin. Silver and pearl can read grey.
  • Gradual tan works on brown skin, subtly. It evens tone more than it darkens.

Mixed evidence

Where glow comes from

Smooth, hydrated skin reflects light like a polished surface. Dry skin scatters it. So the base of any glow is a good moisturiser on damp skin, then a thin layer of oil on shoulders, collarbones and shins. That is often all a photograph needs.

Limited evidence

Shimmer lotions and oils

  • They contain mica or similar mineral particles.
  • Choose warm tones: gold, bronze, copper, rose gold.
  • Fine shimmer looks like skin. Chunky glitter looks like glitter.
  • Apply after moisturiser, to the high points only.
  • It transfers to clothes and sheets.

Mixed evidence

Gradual tan on brown skin

Gradual tanning lotions use DHA, a sugar that reacts with the outer layer of skin to make a brown colour. On already-brown skin the change is small. Some people use it to even out tan lines, lighter patches or legs that are paler than their arms. It gives no sun protection. Patch test: the result can pull orange on some skin tones.

Strong evidence

What to skip

'Glow' is also a marketing word for skin lightening. Products promising a 'brighter', 'fairer' or 'glowing' complexion from informal sellers may contain steroids or other illegal lighteners. Glow from hydration and shimmer washes off. Anything that changes your colour and stays changed is a different kind of product.

When to see a doctor

A rash after a new shimmer or tanning product, or skin colour that changes after using a 'glow' cream.

Common questions

How do I get glowing skin as a Black woman or man?

Moisturise on damp skin, then a thin layer of oil on the high points. Add warm-toned shimmer if you want more.

Does gradual tan work on dark skin?

It works subtly, and is mostly used to even out tone.
