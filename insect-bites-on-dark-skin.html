Bed bug and insect bites on dark skin

How bed bug and other insect bites look on Black and brown skin, why they leave dark marks, how to tell them apart, and how to treat them.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • On darker skin, bites often look brown or purple, not red, and can be hard to see.
  • Bed bug bites come in lines or clusters on skin left uncovered in bed.
  • The itch passes in days. The dark mark it leaves can last months, so do not scratch.

Strong evidence

What they look like

The NHS describes bedbug bites on black or brown skin as brown, red or purple, and harder to see than on white skin. They are raised and itchy, and often sit in a line or a group on the face, neck, arms or other skin exposed during sleep.

Mixed evidence

Telling bites apart

  • Bed bugs: lines or clusters, on exposed skin, new ones in the morning.
  • Fleas: small bites around the ankles and lower legs.
  • Mosquitoes: single, puffy bumps, often outdoors or after travel.
  • Scabies: intense itching at night, between the fingers and at the wrists. Not a bite, and it needs treatment.

Strong evidence

Treating bites

  • A cool, damp cloth, and keep the area clean.
  • Do not scratch. A pharmacist can suggest a mild steroid cream or an antihistamine for the itch.
  • Bites usually clear within a week.

Strong evidence

The marks they leave

Any inflammation in melanin-rich skin can leave a dark mark afterwards. With bites, scratching makes it worse. The marks fade by themselves over weeks to months; sunscreen on exposed areas and not picking are what help most.

Mixed evidence

Getting rid of bed bugs

Look for small dark spots on the mattress seams and bed frame. Wash bedding hot and tumble dry. An infestation usually needs your council or a pest controller.

When to see a doctor

Bites that are hot, swollen, spreading or oozing, or trouble breathing or swelling of the lips after a bite, which is an emergency.

Common questions

What do bed bug bites look like on black skin?

Small raised bumps that look brown, dark red or purple, often in a line or cluster.

Do bites leave dark marks on dark skin?

Often, yes. They fade over weeks to months, faster if you do not scratch.
