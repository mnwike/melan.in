Research / Razor bumps

Pseudofolliculitis barbae; current treatment options

Adebola Ogunbiyi · Clinical, Cosmetic and Investigational Dermatology, 2019

A plain-language summary by melan.in. Not written or endorsed by the authors.

What they did

A review of why razor bumps form and of every treatment in use, from shaving technique to laser.

What they found

  • The cause is a sharp, curved hair re-entering the skin after shaving or plucking.
  • A variant in one hair keratin gene raises the risk about sixfold.
  • Leaving the hair slightly long and avoiding close, dry shaves prevents most cases.

Why it matters

Razor bumps are often dismissed as a grooming problem. This review treats them as a medical condition with a known mechanism and practical fixes.

Limits

A narrative review by one author. Few treatments have been tested in large trials.
