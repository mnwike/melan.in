Mineral oil and petrolatum

Forms a film that stops water escaping from skin or hair.

General information from cosmetic science. Not yet clinically reviewed.

Well established

What the science says

Cosmetic grades are highly refined and among the most effective ingredients for sealing in moisture.

Good to know

Does not add water itself: apply over damp skin.

Cancer evidence

Crude, untreated mineral oils cause skin cancer and are classed as known carcinogens. The highly refined grades allowed in cosmetics are not classed that way and have a long safety record.

Other known downsides

Sits on the surface. Can feel greasy and build up on hair.

Dry and ashy skin

How it appears on a label

Look for: paraffinum liquidum, mineral oil, petrolatum, paraffin.
