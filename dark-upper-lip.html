Dark upper lip and darkening around the mouth

A dark shadow above the lip or around the mouth is common in brown and black skin. The causes, from melasma to hair removal, and the treatments that are safe.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Darkening above the lip is usually melasma, or pigment left by waxing, threading or shaving.
  • Darkness around the whole mouth is often inherited, or from irritation such as lip licking or a product.
  • Sun protection and stopping the irritation come before any lightening treatment.

Mixed evidence

Causes

  • Melasma: symmetrical brown patches driven by hormones and light. Common in pregnancy and on hormonal contraception.
  • Hair removal: repeated waxing, threading or bleaching inflames the skin.
  • Shaving and ingrown hairs in men.
  • Irritation: lip licking, toothpaste, lip products, or a moustache of dried saliva in children.
  • Inherited darker skin around the mouth, especially in South Asian and Black skin.
  • Smoking darkens lips and gums.

Mixed evidence

What helps

  • Daily sunscreen, ideally tinted, right up to the lip line.
  • Switch hair removal method. Laser suited to dark skin reduces repeated trauma.
  • Azelaic acid, niacinamide, or a prescribed short course of a lightening cream for melasma.
  • Petroleum jelly around the mouth if the cause is irritation.
  • Ask a doctor about your contraception if melasma began after starting it.

Strong evidence

What to avoid

Facial bleach, lemon, and lightening creams from informal sellers. The skin around the mouth is thin, and irritation there reliably makes it darker.

When to see a doctor

Darkening inside the mouth or on the gums and lips with tiredness or weight loss, or patches that are spreading.

Common questions

Why is my upper lip dark?

Most often melasma or pigment left by hair removal. Sun and irritation make it worse.
