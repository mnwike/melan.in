Research / Dark marks

Postinflammatory hyperpigmentation: a review of the epidemiology, clinical features, and treatment options in skin of color

Erica Davis, Valerie Callender · Journal of Clinical and Aesthetic Dermatology, 2010

A plain-language summary by melan.in. Not written or endorsed by the authors.

What they did

A review of what was known about dark marks left by inflammation or injury in darker skin: how common they are, what they look like and how they are treated.

What they found

  • Dark marks follow acne especially often in darker skin. In one study about 65% of African-American acne patients developed them.
  • Nine in ten people with razor bumps reported dark marks.
  • Treatment starts with controlling the cause, then sun protection and pigment-fading creams.

Why it matters

It is the paper to read if you want to understand why marks linger and which treatments have evidence. It is free to read.

Limits

A review of earlier studies, many of them small. Some newer treatments are not covered.
