Body lotions with retinol, acids and vitamin C

Body lotions with retinol, lactic acid, glycolic acid, salicylic acid, niacinamide and vitamin C: which help dark marks and rough skin on darker skin, safely.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Active body lotions can smooth rough skin and slowly fade marks on the body.
  • On darker skin the main risk is irritation, which causes new dark marks. Start low and slow.
  • Lactic acid and niacinamide are the gentlest starting points.

Mixed evidence

What each one does

  • Lactic acid: moisturises and gently loosens dead cells. Good for dry, bumpy arms and legs.
  • Glycolic acid: stronger exfoliation. More results, more irritation.
  • Salicylic acid: gets into pores. For body acne and ingrown hairs.
  • Retinol: speeds skin turnover. For texture, stretch marks that are still new, and marks. Not in pregnancy.
  • Niacinamide: calms and slows pigment transfer. Gentle.
  • Vitamin C: antioxidant. Body formulas are often too weak or unstable to do much.

Limited evidence

Using them on Black and brown skin

  • One active at a time.
  • Two or three nights a week to begin with, then build.
  • Moisturise over the top.
  • Sunscreen on any exposed treated skin. Acids and retinol make skin more sun-sensitive.
  • Stop if it burns, peels or darkens. Pushing through causes marks.
  • Not on freshly shaved or waxed skin.

Mixed evidence

What to expect

Smoother texture in two to four weeks. Fading of marks on the body takes three months or more, and body marks fade more slowly than facial ones. Bumpy upper arms improve while you use the lotion and return when you stop.

Strong evidence

What these are not

None of these is a skin lightener. They even out marks and texture towards your own skin tone. A body lotion that visibly lightens your overall colour within weeks contains something that should not be in it.

When to see a doctor

Burning, blistering, new dark or light patches after starting a product, or body acne that leaves marks.

Common questions

Is retinol body lotion safe for dark skin?

Yes, used gradually with moisturiser and sun protection. Irritation is the risk, because it can leave dark marks.

Which body lotion is best for dark spots on legs?

A lactic acid or niacinamide lotion is the gentlest place to start. Expect three months.
