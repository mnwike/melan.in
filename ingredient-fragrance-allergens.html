What the science says

Each is a recognised contact allergen for a minority of people, which is why they must be named.

Worth knowing

UK and EU rules require these to be listed by name above a set level.

How it appears on a label

Look for: limonene, linalool, citral, coumarin, hexyl cinnamal, benzyl salicylate, benzyl benzoate, geraniol.
