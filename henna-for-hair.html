Henna for hair

Henna dyes hair red-orange and coats the strand. How it works on grey and on curly hair, why it can loosen curls, and why 'black henna' is dangerous.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Pure henna stains hair a red-orange shade. It cannot lighten hair or dye it black by itself.
  • It coats and stiffens the strand, which can loosen curl pattern and dry out some hair.
  • 'Black henna' usually contains PPD, a chemical dye that causes severe skin reactions.

Strong evidence

What henna is

Henna is the powdered leaf of a shrub grown across North Africa, the Middle East and South Asia, used on hair, skin and nails for thousands of years. Its pigment, lawsone, binds to keratin. On dark hair it gives a reddish sheen. On grey hair it gives bright copper-orange. Mixed with indigo it gives browns and near-black.

Limited evidence

On curly and afro-textured hair

  • It adds weight and a protein-like coating, so some people find strands feel thicker and stronger.
  • The same coating commonly loosens the curl pattern, sometimes permanently.
  • Hair can feel dry and stiff afterwards. Deep condition after rinsing.
  • It is permanent, and chemical dye or bleach over henna can behave unpredictably.
  • Do a strand test on shed hair first.

Strong evidence

Safety

  • Buy body-art quality henna with a single ingredient.
  • 'Black henna' and many boxed 'henna' dyes contain PPD. Reactions can be severe and leave scars, and they sensitise you to hair dye for life.
  • Some henna powders have been found to contain metallic salts or added dyes.
  • People with G6PD deficiency, which is more common in people of African, Mediterranean and South Asian ancestry, should avoid henna, and it should not be used on babies.

When to see a doctor

Itching, swelling, blisters or weeping after henna or a 'black henna' tattoo, on the scalp or skin.

Common questions

Is henna good for hair?

It colours and coats hair, which some people like. It can loosen curls and dry hair, and black henna is unsafe.
