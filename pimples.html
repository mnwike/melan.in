Pimples: how to get rid of them

What causes pimples, what clears them, how to stop them leaving dark marks on brown and black skin, and why toothpaste, lemon and squeezing make things worse.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • A pimple is a blocked, inflamed pore. It needs two to three months of steady treatment, not an overnight fix.
  • On brown and black skin every pimple can leave a dark mark, so prevention matters more than spot treatment.
  • Squeezing and home remedies are the main causes of marks and scars.

Strong evidence

What works

  • Benzoyl peroxide 2.5 to 5%: kills acne bacteria. Start every other day.
  • Adapalene, a retinoid: unblocks pores and fades marks. At night.
  • Salicylic acid cleansers for oily skin.
  • Azelaic acid: treats pimples and marks together.
  • A GP can add antibiotics, hormonal treatment or isotretinoin for severe acne.

Mixed evidence

Stopping the marks

  • Do not squeeze, pick or scrub.
  • Treat the whole area, not single spots.
  • Moisturiser and sunscreen every day, even on oily skin.
  • A pimple patch stops you touching it.

Mixed evidence

What makes them worse

  • Toothpaste, lemon, garlic and baking soda: burn and darken skin.
  • Steroid creams and fairness creams: cause a severe acne of their own.
  • Heavy hair oils and pomades running onto the forehead.
  • Thick cocoa butter or coconut oil on the face.
  • Whey protein and high-sugar diets in some people.

When to see a doctor

Deep painful lumps, scarring, pimples that started after a lightening cream, or no improvement after three months.

Common questions

How do I get rid of pimples fast?

Nothing clears them overnight. Benzoyl peroxide or adapalene used daily clears most within two to three months.
