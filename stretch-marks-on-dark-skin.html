Stretch marks on Black and brown skin

Stretch marks on dark skin start dark brown or purple and fade lighter than your skin. What causes them, what the trials say about cocoa butter, and what helps.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • New stretch marks on dark skin are dark brown, purple or reddish-brown. Old ones turn paler than your skin.
  • They are scars in the deeper layer of skin. Nothing rubbed on the surface removes them.
  • Cocoa butter has been tested in proper trials, including one in Jamaica. It did not prevent them.

Strong evidence

What they are

When skin stretches faster than its deeper layer can adapt, in a growth spurt, pregnancy, weight change or muscle gain, the collagen tears and heals as a line. Hormones matter too, which is why steroid tablets and strong steroid creams can cause them.

Mixed evidence

Colour on darker skin

Fresh marks carry more pigment and blood flow, so they look darker than your skin. Over a year or two they flatten and lose pigment, ending lighter, sometimes with a silvery shine. On dark skin that contrast is why older marks stay visible.

Mixed evidence

A warning sign

Stretch marks in unusual places such as the inner thighs, groin, armpits or face, in someone using a 'lightening' or 'toning' cream, suggest the cream contains a strong steroid. Stop and see a doctor.

Strong evidence

What the evidence says

  • Cocoa butter: randomised trials, including one in Afro-Caribbean women in Jamaica, found no difference from a placebo cream.
  • Olive oil and most 'stretch mark oils': no good evidence of prevention.
  • Moisturising makes skin more comfortable and less itchy. That is a fair reason to do it.
  • Prescription tretinoin can improve new, still-dark marks. It must not be used in pregnancy.
  • Lasers and microneedling can soften marks. In darker skin they risk dark or light patches, so choose someone experienced with skin of colour and ask for a test area.

Mixed evidence

A fair expectation

Most stretch marks fade a great deal by themselves. Most adults have some. Treatment can soften them. It does not erase them.

When to see a doctor

Wide purple marks appearing quickly along with weight gain around the middle, a rounder face or easy bruising, or marks appearing while using steroid creams or tablets.

Common questions

What colour are stretch marks on black skin?

Dark brown, purple or reddish-brown when new, fading to lighter than the surrounding skin.

Does cocoa butter prevent stretch marks?

Randomised trials found it worked no better than a placebo cream.
