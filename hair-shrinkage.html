Shrinkage in afro and curly hair

Shrinkage is tightly coiled hair returning to its natural shape as it dries. Why it happens, why it signals healthy hair, and how to stretch hair safely.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Shrinkage is normal. Coily hair can look a quarter of its real length.
  • Hair that shrinks has good elasticity. Hair that no longer shrinks is often damaged.
  • Stretch with braids, twists and banding before reaching for heat.

Strong evidence

Why it happens

A hair fibre is held in shape by chemical bonds. Water breaks the weak hydrogen bonds, so wet hair can be pulled long. As it dries the bonds reform and the fibre returns to the curl set by the shape of its follicle. The tighter the coil, the bigger the difference between stretched and resting length. In type 4 hair that difference is commonly 50 to 75%.

Mixed evidence

What it tells you

  • Springing back after a gentle pull means the fibre is elastic and intact.
  • Sections that stay limp and straight when wet are usually heat or chemical damage.
  • Shrinkage is not slow growth. Afro-textured hair grows a little more slowly than other types on average, but most 'not growing' is breakage and shrinkage together.

Limited evidence

Stretching without heat

  • Twist-outs and braid-outs on damp hair.
  • Banding: hair ties placed along a ponytail while it dries.
  • African threading, a traditional method across West Africa.
  • Bantu knots.
  • Roller or flexi-rod sets.
  • Drying in large plaits after washing.

Mixed evidence

Stretching with heat

  • A blow-dryer on low or medium with a comb attachment, on hair that is mostly dry.
  • Always a heat protectant.
  • Keep straighteners occasional. Repeated high heat permanently loosens the curl.

When to see a doctor

Hair that snaps at the same length, bald patches, or a sore scalp. That is breakage or hair loss, not shrinkage.

Common questions

Is shrinkage a sign of healthy hair?

Usually yes. It shows the hair is elastic.

How much does 4C hair shrink?

Commonly by half to three quarters of its stretched length.
