Privacy notice

What happens to your details when you sign up or send us a question.

Last updated November 2026

Who we are

melan.in is an independent library about Black and brown skin and hair. For anything about your data, email martin@nwike.com.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address, when you fill in the sign-up form.
  • Your phone number, only if you choose to give it.
  • Anything you write in the question box.
  • The page you were on and the time you ticked the consent box.

We do not use advertising trackers. The site remembers in your own browser that you have seen the sign-up box, so it does not show again.

Why, and on what basis

We use your details to send you the news, routines, product information and offers you asked for, and to reply to your question. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box. You can withdraw it at any time.

Please do not send detailed medical information in the question box. We are not a medical service and cannot give personal medical advice.

Who handles it

The form is delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, a form-to-email service, and then held in our email account. We do not sell your details or share them with brands, clinics or anyone else without asking you first.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Questions are kept for up to two years.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or to stop hearing from us, by emailing martin@nwike.com. If you are unhappy with how we respond you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

melan.in gathers and explains published research. It is information, not medical advice. For a concern about your own skin or hair, see a doctor.

Every claim on this site links to its source.
