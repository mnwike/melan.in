Research / How hair ages

Hair aging in different races and ethnicities

Mayra Maymone, Neelam Vashi · Journal of Clinical and Aesthetic Dermatology, 2021

A plain-language summary by melan.in. Not written or endorsed by the authors.

What they did

A review of 69 published papers on how hair changes with age in people of different ancestries.

What they found

  • Greying begins on average in the mid-thirties in people of European ancestry, the late thirties in Asian ancestry and the mid-forties in African ancestry.
  • In European and Asian hair, damage sits at the far ends. In African American hair, it sits nearer the root.
  • After the menopause hair grows more slowly and becomes finer.

Why it matters

It gives clinicians and readers a basis for age-specific hair advice that is not copied from European hair.

Limits

A summary of other studies, many of them small. 'Average age' hides a wide spread, and greying is strongly inherited.
