Chemical peels on Black and brown skin

Chemical peels can help acne, melasma and dark marks on darker skin, but the wrong peel causes burns and pigment change. Which peels suit, and what to ask.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Superficial peels are generally safe on darker skin in experienced hands.
  • Medium and deep peels carry a high risk of lasting dark or light patches and are usually avoided.
  • The person matters more than the product. Ask how often they treat skin like yours.

Strong evidence

What a peel is

A chemical peel is a controlled injury. An acid is applied to remove a set depth of skin, which then heals. Superficial peels affect only the outer layer. Medium peels reach the upper dermis and deep peels go further.

Mixed evidence

Peels commonly used on skin of colour

  • Salicylic acid 20 to 30%: acne and oily skin.
  • Mandelic acid: gentle, for marks and acne.
  • Lactic acid: dry or sensitive skin.
  • Glycolic acid 20 to 35%: melasma and marks, with short contact times.
  • Jessner's solution and low-strength TCA: only with specialists experienced in darker skin.

Mixed evidence

What good practice looks like

  • A consultation first, with questions about cold sores, keloids, recent isotretinoin and past dark marks.
  • Skin preparation for two to four weeks, often with a pigment-calming cream and sunscreen.
  • A test patch.
  • A course of several mild peels, not one strong one.
  • Written aftercare and a number to call.

Strong evidence

Risks

  • Dark marks after the peel are the commonest problem in darker skin.
  • Light patches, which can be permanent.
  • Burns and scarring, including keloids in people prone to them.
  • Cold sore flare-ups.
  • Peel kits sold online at clinic strength have caused serious burns. Do not use them at home.

When to see a doctor

Blistering, oozing, severe pain, or patches of colour change after a peel. Go back to the clinic, or to a GP if the clinic is not helpful.

Common questions

Are chemical peels safe for Black skin?

Superficial peels usually are when done by someone experienced with darker skin. Deep peels are generally avoided.
