Hair porosity: low and high porosity explained

What hair porosity means, how to tell low porosity from high porosity hair, why the float test is unreliable, and how to care for each.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Porosity is how easily water moves in and out of a strand. It depends on the state of the cuticle.
  • It reflects the condition of the hair more than a fixed type. Bleach, heat and wear raise it.
  • The glass-of-water float test is unreliable. How your hair behaves when wet is a better guide.

Mixed evidence

What it is

Cosmetic chemists describe low porosity hair as having cuticle layers that lie flat and resist water and treatments. In high porosity hair the cuticle is lifted or worn by chemical, heat or mechanical stress, so water enters fast and leaves fast. One head can hold a mix.

Limited evidence

Signs to look for

  • Low porosity: water beads on the surface, hair takes a long time to get fully wet and a long time to dry, products sit on top.
  • High porosity: hair soaks up water at once, dries quickly, feels dry, frizzes and breaks more easily.

Limited evidence

Caring for each

  • Low porosity: lighter products, applied to damp hair. Clear build-up regularly.
  • High porosity: conditioner every wash, a leave-in, less heat and gentle handling. The aim is to protect a worn surface.

Limited evidence

How sure is any of this?

Porosity is a real property that laboratories measure. Most consumer advice built on it has not been tested in trials, and guides often contradict each other. Treat it as one clue alongside curl type and how your hair responds.

When to see a doctor

Porosity is not a medical matter. Sudden changes in hair texture with shedding are worth mentioning to a GP.

Common questions

How do I know if I have low porosity hair?

Water beads on it, it is slow to get fully wet and slow to dry, and products tend to sit on the surface.

Does the float test work?

It is unreliable. Oils, product and trapped air change whether a strand floats.
