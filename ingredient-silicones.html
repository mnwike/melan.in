Ingredients / Conditioning agent

Silicones

Coats the hair to add slip and shine and reduce friction.

General information from cosmetic science. Not yet clinically reviewed.

Well established

What the science says

Well studied and considered safe. Some build up on hair and need a cleansing shampoo to remove.

Worth knowing

Avoided in some curly-hair routines because of build-up, not because of safety.

Cancer evidence

No established cancer link. The cyclic silicone D4 is suspected of harming fertility and, with D5, is restricted in the UK and EU, largely because both build up in the environment.

Other known downsides

Can build up on hair and weigh it down if not washed out.

How it appears on a label

Look for: dimethicone, siloxane, amodimethicone, dimethiconol, trimethicone, cyclomethicone.
