Guides / The big conversations

Can you increase melanin?

Foods and supplements do not raise melanin. What does change it, why 'melanin pills' and tanning injections are unsafe, and what helps pigment loss.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • No food, vitamin or pill has been shown to increase melanin in healthy skin or hair.
  • Sun exposure raises it, at the cost of skin damage. Tanning injections are unlicensed and unsafe.
  • Loss of pigment, as in vitiligo or grey hair, has real treatments. Those are a doctor's job.

Strong evidence

What sets your melanin

The amount and type of melanin you make is set mainly by your genes. It shifts with sunlight, hormones such as those of pregnancy, inflammation and age. Everyone has roughly the same number of pigment cells. They differ in how active those cells are.

Mixed evidence

Foods and supplements

Lists of 'melanin-boosting foods' rest on the fact that pigment cells use the amino acid tyrosine, copper and certain vitamins. Lacking them can reduce pigment. Having more than enough does not increase it. Severe deficiencies of vitamin B12, copper or protein can lighten hair or skin, and correcting them restores colour. For everyone else, a normal diet already supplies what is needed.

Strong evidence

Sun, sunbeds and injections

Ultraviolet light does increase melanin: a tan is the skin's response to DNA damage. Sunbeds do the same with a higher cancer risk. 'Melanotan' tanning injections and nasal sprays sold online are unlicensed, have been linked to changes in moles and other harms, and regulators warn against them.

Mixed evidence

If you are losing pigment

White patches from vitiligo can often be partly repigmented with prescription creams and light therapy. Light patches after eczema or a rash return slowly once the rash is controlled. Grey hair cannot be reversed except in the rare case of a correctable deficiency.

When to see a doctor

Spreading white patches, sudden loss of colour in skin or hair, or any change after using a tanning injection or spray.

Common questions

Which foods increase melanin?

None have been shown to in healthy people. Only correcting a real deficiency restores lost pigment.
