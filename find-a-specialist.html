Find a specialist

Who to see for skin, hair and scalp, where to search for someone experienced with darker skin and afro hair, and what to ask before you book.

Directory links checked November 2026 · We do not vet individual practitioners

The short answer

  • For a medical problem with skin, hair or scalp, the specialist is a dermatologist: a doctor with years of extra training.
  • A trichologist is a hair and scalp specialist who is usually not a doctor. Good ones spot problems early and refer on.
  • You are looking for experience with darker skin and afro hair. That is not the same as the practitioner's own skin colour.

Questions to ask before you book

  • How often do you treat patients with skin or hair like mine?
  • Have you treated this condition in darker skin, and what did you use?
  • For a dermatologist in the UK: are you on the GMC specialist register for dermatology?
  • For a trichologist: which register are you on, and when do you refer to a doctor?
  • What will this cost, and what happens if it does not work?

Why we do not rank individual doctors

Seniority and publication counts do not tell you who will care for you well, and a ranking we could not keep accurate would mislead. Our people pages profile researchers by what they have published. For choosing a clinician, use the registers above and the questions.
