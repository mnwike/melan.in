Acne on Black and brown skin

Acne in darker skin leaves dark marks that often bother people more than the spots. What causes it, what treats both, and which products make it worse.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • In darker skin the dark mark left behind often outlasts and outweighs the spot.
  • Treat early and gently. Irritated skin makes more pigment.
  • Retinoids treat both the acne and the marks, and are a first choice in skin of colour.

Strong evidence

What is different

Reviews of acne in skin of colour make the same point: the hyperpigmentation acne leaves can be more distressing than the acne itself, and even mild spots can cause it. Treating acne quickly is the best way to prevent marks.

Strong evidence

What treats it

  • A retinoid at night, such as adapalene from a pharmacy. Trials show they reduce both spots and pigmentation in skin of colour.
  • Benzoyl peroxide for inflamed spots. It can bleach fabric.
  • Azelaic acid: treats spots and marks, and is gentle.
  • Sunscreen every morning, so marks fade instead of deepening.
  • From a doctor: stronger retinoids, antibiotics, hormonal treatment, or isotretinoin for severe or scarring acne.

Mixed evidence

Go gently

Retinoids can irritate, and irritation leaves its own marks. Start with a low strength, a few nights a week, with moisturiser, and build up. Do not scrub, and do not pick.

Mixed evidence

Hair products and the forehead

Spots clustered on the forehead and temples are often 'pomade acne', caused by oils and butters from hair products reaching the skin. Keep heavy products off the hairline, and cover hair or wash your face after applying them.

Mixed evidence

What to avoid

  • Lightening creams for the marks without a prescription.
  • Harsh scrubs, lemon juice, toothpaste.
  • Stopping treatment as soon as the spots clear. Acne needs maintenance.

When to see a doctor

Deep, painful lumps, scarring, keloids forming on the chest, jaw or shoulders, or acne that has not improved after three months of pharmacy treatment.

Common questions

How do you treat acne on black skin?

A retinoid, benzoyl peroxide or azelaic acid, plus daily sunscreen, started early and built up gently.

Why does acne leave dark marks on dark skin?

Inflammation makes pigment cells in melanin-rich skin produce extra melanin, which stays after the spot heals.
