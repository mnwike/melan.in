High porosity hair

High porosity hair absorbs water fast and loses it fast. What causes it, the signs, and the protein, sealing and gentle handling that help.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • High porosity hair soaks up water quickly and dries quickly.
  • It is usually caused by damage: bleach, relaxer, heat, sun or rough handling.
  • It needs protein to patch the gaps and heavier products to seal.

Mixed evidence

What it means

In high porosity hair the cuticle scales are lifted, chipped or missing, so water rushes in and out. Hair swells, tangles, frizzes in humidity and feels dry soon after moisturising. The oldest hair, at the ends, is usually the most porous.

Strong evidence

Causes

  • Bleach and permanent colour.
  • Relaxers and texturisers.
  • Frequent flat ironing or blow drying.
  • Sun, chlorine and sea water.
  • Years of combing and friction.

Limited evidence

What suits it

  • Protein treatments every few weeks, followed by moisture.
  • Leave-in conditioner, then a cream, then an oil or butter to seal.
  • Coconut oil before washing can reduce protein loss.
  • Cool or lukewarm rinses and gentle detangling.
  • Limit further heat and chemical processing.
  • Trim the most damaged ends.

When to see a doctor

Porosity is a care question, not a medical one. See a doctor for hair loss or scalp symptoms.

Common questions

What causes high porosity hair?

Mostly damage from bleach, relaxers, heat and sun, which lifts or strips the cuticle.
