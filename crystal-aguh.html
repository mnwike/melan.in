Dermatologist · Johns Hopkins University, USA

This profile points to published work. Dr Crystal Aguh is not affiliated with melan.in and has not reviewed this page.

Known for

Researches hair loss in Black women. Her review with Alessandra Haskin ranked common hairstyles by their risk of traction alopecia and gave practical limits for wearing them.
