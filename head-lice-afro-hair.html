Can Black people get head lice?

Yes, afro hair can get head lice, though it is less common. Why, how to check coils, braids and locs, and how to treat lice without wrecking the hair.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Yes. Head lice are less common in afro-textured hair, not impossible.
  • Itching is far more often a dry or flaky scalp. Confirm lice before treating.
  • Wet combing with lots of conditioner finds and removes them, and suits coily hair.

Mixed evidence

Why it is less common

The US Centers for Disease Control says infestation is much less common among African American people, probably because the louse found most often in the US has claws suited to gripping some hair shapes and widths better than others. That is a pattern in one country, not immunity. Believing Black children cannot get lice delays the diagnosis and lets it spread.

Mixed evidence

How to check

  • Wet the hair and load it with conditioner. It slows the lice down.
  • Work in small sections with a fine detection comb, wiping it on white tissue after each pass.
  • Look hardest at the temples, behind the ears and the nape.
  • You are looking for a live, moving louse. Flakes, lint and product are not lice.

Limited evidence

Braids, locs and extensions

Check along the partings and the hairline. If you cannot get a comb to the scalp, loose styles may need to come out to treat properly. Locs cannot be wet combed: ask a pharmacist about a silicone-based lotion that works by coating the lice.

Mixed evidence

Treating it

  • Wet combing every few days for about two weeks, until three sessions in a row find nothing.
  • Or a pharmacy lotion based on a silicone such as dimeticone. Research supports these as effective and safe.
  • Check everyone in the household. Treat only those with live lice.
  • You do not need to cut the hair, and lice have nothing to do with cleanliness.

When to see a doctor

A scalp that is sore, weeping or crusted from scratching, or lice that survive two full rounds of treatment.

Common questions

Can Black people get lice?

Yes. It is less common in afro-textured hair but it does happen.

How do you get rid of lice in afro hair?

Wet comb with plenty of conditioner every few days for two weeks, or use a silicone-based pharmacy lotion.
