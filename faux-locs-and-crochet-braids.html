Faux locs, butterfly locs and crochet braids

Faux locs, butterfly locs, goddess locs and crochet styles. How they are installed, how heavy they are, how long to keep them, and protecting your edges.

Not yet clinically reviewed · Last updated November 2026

The short answer

  • Crochet styles are quicker and often lighter than individual braids.
  • Wrapped faux locs can be heavy. Weight and tightness are what damage follicles.
  • Six to eight weeks is the limit, then give the hair a rest.

Strong evidence

The styles

  • Crochet braids: hair is cornrowed flat and pre-made hair is looped through with a latch hook.
  • Faux locs: your hair is braided or twisted, then wrapped with synthetic or human hair to look like locs.
  • Butterfly locs: wrapped loosely so loops stand out, giving a distressed look.
  • Goddess locs: faux locs with loose curly ends.
  • Soft locs: pre-made lightweight locs crocheted in and wrapped at the root.

Mixed evidence

The risks

  • Weight: long wrapped locs pull constantly on the root, most at the hairline and nape.
  • Tight cornrows under crochet hair.
  • Itching: many people react to the alkaline coating on synthetic hair. Soaking it in diluted apple cider vinegar and rinsing before use often helps.
  • Matting if left in too long, which leads to cutting on take-down.

Mixed evidence

Looking after them

  • Choose shorter, lighter locs if your edges are already thin.
  • Cleanse the scalp every one to two weeks with diluted shampoo in a nozzle bottle. Dry thoroughly, because wrapped hair holds water and can smell.
  • Do not re-do the front rows to 'freshen' the style. That doubles the tension on the weakest hair.
  • Take down slowly, cut the wrap below your own hair length, and detangle with conditioner before washing.
  • Leave at least two weeks before the next install.

When to see a doctor

Pain, bumps or pus along the hairline or partings, a widening parting, or patches where hair is not growing back. Take the style out and see a GP or dermatologist.

Common questions

How long do faux locs last?

Six to eight weeks at most.

Why do crochet braids itch?

Often a reaction to the coating on synthetic hair. Rinsing the hair in diluted vinegar before install can help.
