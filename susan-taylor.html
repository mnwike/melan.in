Dr Susan C. Taylor

Dermatologist · University of Pennsylvania, USA

This profile points to published work. Dr Susan C. Taylor is not affiliated with melan.in and has not reviewed this page.

Known for

Founded the Skin of Color Society in 2004 and is lead editor of the textbook Dermatology for Skin of Color. Her work helped establish skin of colour as a field of study in its own right, from pigment disorders to hair and scalp conditions in children and adults.
